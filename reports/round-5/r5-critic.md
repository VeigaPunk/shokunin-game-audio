# Round 5 critic — calibration claim falsification

## Scope and evidence

I reviewed the round-5 claim/spec in `.shokunin/audio/LOG.md`, the current fixture checker and its stated rules in `corpus/knowledge/2026-10-12-fixture-battery.md`, and the latest clean and probe receipts under `corpus/live/runs/` (clean `20261012T164320494590Z-normal`; flat `164318673149Z-flat`; slope `164319311023Z-slope`; broken-amplitude `164319899564Z-failing`). This is a source/receipt critique, not an independent decode or rebuild. No shell commands were run.

## Falsification / limits

- Ten clean WAVs are ten deliberately authored, deterministic fixtures, not a sample of production assets, users, codecs, or operating conditions. Several are paired variants, and the 30 Hz section fixture is analytically constructed. Min/max across them are **observed fixture values**, not an estimate of a population distribution, calibration curve, error rate, confidence interval, or evidence that a bound is well chosen. Repeated receipts of the same deterministic build do not add independent samples.
- A clean value's distance to a threshold is a numerical margin for this file, not a safety margin or proof of adequate tolerance. Do not describe margins as calibrated headroom. Some bounds are one-sided, some inclusive/exclusive, some exact format requirements; report the code's polarity verbatim. Duration tolerance is `> .001` failure (equality passes); peak fails at `>= .95`, RMS at `< .001`; centroid bounds are inclusive; endpoint/slope fail only at `> .03`/`> .001`; section contrast passes at `>= 6 dB` after validity floors. The decision must not imply these boundaries were empirically selected by the observed clean ranges.
- Available controls are selective. `flat30_probe` exercises the section contrast check at 0 dB while its constituent amplitudes/RMS remain valid; `slope_probe` isolates slope failure with matching endpoints; the old broken-amplitude probe changes ambience centroid **and** endpoint jump, so it does not isolate either bound. These controls do not exercise format, duration, peak, RMS floor/ceiling, any centroid-band edge, distinctness threshold, section amplitude/RMS floors, or an ebur128 null interpretation. For those rows say **“not exercised by a negative control”**, not “validated,” “calibrated,” or “proven.”
- Existing flat/slope controls do not establish detection probability near a boundary: their values are deliberately far from 6 dB / .001, respectively. They show a selected case is rejected, not sensitivity/specificity, robustness, or correct operation at the threshold. The broken control's multiple simultaneous failures similarly prevents attributing its result to one rule.
- The report's intended claim must preserve the distinction between the ten-file clean range and the available probe results. A table that puts control outcomes in a distribution column or combines clean and negative-probe samples would be misleading. Label each source set and each control's targeted check separately.
- The loudness mechanism is especially easy to overstate. For the latest clean receipt, UI/impact/movement and their alternatives have `integrated_lufs: null`; ambience, ambience_alt, stem, stem_sections report −30.7, −24.7, −15.8, −24.7 LUFS. The script explicitly maps nonfinite or `<= -70` ebur128 output to null. Null is a gated/undefined measurement under this implementation, not a loudness value, silence, or a pass/fail loudness target. The four finite values do not define an acceptance window. State that no loudness acceptance window is declared and use RMS dBFS only as a separate reported level metric; do not call the fixture LUFS range calibrated.
- Format checking only observes the clean files' expected format and does not test a wrong-format control. Duration checking similarly sees exact authored durations but no tolerance-edge or failing duration. The same criticism applies even if the table has computed margins for every clean item.
- The section rule is an exact-bin 30 Hz component proxy on two fixed halves. Its threshold and control concern this proxy only; they do not validate a general section-change detector or establish motif, musical development, audibility, or quality. Centroid bands and SFX distinctness likewise remain local analytic checks, not perceptual calibration.

## Honest decision-table language

Recommended labels: **“threshold as implemented (local rule; unchanged)”**, **“clean fixture observations (n=10 constructed files; not a population distribution)”**, **“nearest observed clean margin (descriptive arithmetic only)”**, **“negative control (named, targeted check only)”**, **“not exercised”**, and **“interpretation / caveat.”** For per-fixture metrics use ranges only where all relevant files have that metric; identify loop-only or section-only subsets rather than implying ten observations. Keep controls out of clean minima/maxima.

A defensible summary is: *“The table consolidates the existing local thresholds and describes measurements from the current ten clean deterministic WAV fixtures plus separately identified, targeted probes. These observations document this fixture battery; they do not establish population distributions or validate the thresholds as universal or perceptual standards. Negative-control evidence is limited to the checks specifically named; other thresholds were not exercised by a failing control. No LUFS acceptance window is declared.”*

Calibration can be said to improve only in the narrow procedural sense that a reader can now see threshold polarity, observed fixture margin, and whether a matching control exists in one place. Avoid claiming that the values themselves are empirically calibrated, validated across audio, or better tuned.
