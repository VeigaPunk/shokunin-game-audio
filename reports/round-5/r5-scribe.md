## Caveats for integration

- “Nearest margin” is arithmetic distance to the enforced numerical boundary for these ten fixture values; it is not confidence, robustness under codec/gain changes, or a universal safety margin. For OR logic, report distance to either passing branch (as above), not a misleading single threshold.
- Probe names do not imply isolated tests: the old broken-amplitude probe trips centroid and endpoint together. The slope and flat30 controls isolate their intended measured failure in the cited replay. Wrong format, duration, and peak/RMS boundaries have no negative controls in this evidence set.
- For section contrast, implementation and receipts call it `section_energy_delta_db`, but formula is `20*log10(A2/A1)` on component amplitudes; preserve that exact operational definition and avoid claiming a total-energy-normalized metric.
- `r4-verifier-replay.md` is independent raw-artifact replay evidence for these receipts; this proposal does not claim to be a new independent reproduction. No perceptual, mastering, codec/runtime, or rights claim follows.
