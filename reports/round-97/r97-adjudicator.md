# r97 Adjudication - Private WirePlumber Policy

DECISION: **ADMIT-with-findings + required safety fold** for the measured
policy observations at `b8ff4efc`. This does not clear the current
teardown guard for unattended execution or certify shared-state isolation.

## Five-Gate Record

- Verifier: two archived committed-byte batteries reproduce all 69 leg
  keys and 260/260 independent checks. Negative/forgery controls
  discriminate and the recorded private-core exits are clean.
- Critic: measured policy mechanisms survive, but a changed shared
  cross-witness can leave failures empty; error-class text is partly
  constant-fed; the pid probe is zombie-blind; the kill predicate accepts
  a foreign process that merely mentions the private root.
- Reviewer independently corroborates those defects and extends the
  unarmed-hash finding to settings endpoints. The purported shared
  environment retains `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, so it can select a private
  runtime despite stripping other PipeWire variables.
- Scribe confirms copy/wiring custody but rejects the absolute
  no-`pactl` claim. Read-only inventory calls exist; the family-specific
  no-shared-write observation is narrower than a whole-file claim.
- Simplifier proves candidate removal of non-derived fields, dead
  arguments and one duplicate observation. Those candidates change
  bookkeeping laws and are not already-landed simplifications.

The verifier's hardlink write-through incident is retained as disclosed:
the archive was briefly altered, detected by hash, restored and
re-verified. It is not described as an uninterrupted frozen-byte run.

## Required Fold

1. Fail closed on the settings boundary hashes and shared-before/after
   cross-witness. Unavailable witnesses must be named unmeasured; they
   cannot prove isolation.
2. Bind the shared witness to a known shared runtime, including explicit
   treatment of `XDG_RUNTIME_DIR`, rather than trusting inherited env.
3. Restrict teardown signals to positively owned child identities.
   A command-line substring containing a private root is insufficient.
   Test a foreign reader mentioning that root as a must-not-signal case.
   Until this is repaired, do not run this family unattended.
4. Reap child processes and distinguish zombies from live processes.
   Record stop, signal and restart timestamps separately; the current
   grace-period artifact is not a measured restart latency.
5. Derive error classes from observed output or explicitly remove the
   misleading constant-fed fields. The simplifier's candidate is not a
   reason to leave old law-set expectations silently inconsistent.
6. Correct the 69-keys versus 68-distinct-observations distinction,
   no-`pactl` scope, and rc-null/alive-at-cap descriptions. Any removal
   of a duplicate key needs a new declared census.
7. The family lock remains tree-relative and main-dispatch-only.
   Its demonstrated scope is same-tree dispatch exclusion, not
   cross-tree or direct-entry exclusion.

Admit the observed private policy/rank/default/restart-race outcomes;
reject absolute safety/isolation and timing claims contradicted above.
No guard repair, re-pin, law-set rewrite or simplifier deletion
accompanies this report. Evidence and residual gaps remain in all five
`r97-*` wave-B reports in this directory.
