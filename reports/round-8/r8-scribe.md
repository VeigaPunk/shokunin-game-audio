## Numerical caveats and integration advice

- The section estimator is exact-bin and assumes the declared 120 Hz component aligns to bin 240 for each 96,000-frame half. It is narrow-band and not a general estimator for detuned, time-varying, or differently aligned components.
- The contrast rule is signed: section 2 must exceed section 1. A reversed transition does not pass merely because its magnitude difference is large.
- Component amplitude contrast is not normalized total-energy contrast: the common 220/330 Hz terms contribute to section RMS and whole-file centroid, but are excluded from the 120 Hz numerator. The flat control should retain the same common terms to isolate the test.
- PCM16 quantization means `.012`, `.060`, `.036` are synthesis targets, not exact decoded observations. Record actual decoded amplitudes, RMS, contrast, count, and ordinary measurements in the receipt. Avoid presenting target amplitudes as observed measurements.
- Existing `section_contrast()` in the inspected script is hard-coded to 30 Hz/bin 60 and expects exactly 192,000 decoded frames. It cannot directly establish the 120 Hz result without a component-frequency/bin parameter or an equivalent separately implemented estimator. Reuse the count/validation logic and avoid duplicated gates; any integration belongs to the parent, not this report.
- The declared `--probe-layer-flat` mode is not yet evidenced by a run here. The proposed control outcome is a criterion, not a result. Normal / control / restoration receipts and the ten unchanged hashes are verifier territory.
- No listening, engine/runtime, perceptual, security, or rights inference follows from these analytic PCM measurements. No user-sourced or borrowed recording is involved in this proposed design.
