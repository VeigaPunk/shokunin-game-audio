## Limits and follow-up evidence

The proposed 200 Hz addition targets a distinct section-component analysis frequency while reusing existing fixture constraints. The component frequency and `.015/.050` synthesis amplitudes are chosen inputs, not calibrated or perceptual claims. Quantization can make decoded amplitudes differ from those targets. A valid coverage result requires actual decoded component amplitudes and contrast, format/duration/level/centroid and endpoint/slope measurements, internal-join values, the full failure list, candidate-to-builder byte identity, unchanged canonical hashes and prior failure outcomes, and a non-author serial replay. These are separate evidence steps; failed setup is not evidence of a pass. Sol’s named per-axis adjudication remains necessary.

This proposed deterministic-fixture coverage change supports no claim about audible quality, seamlessness, musical value, an adaptive or production engine, security, external JSON-reader compatibility, or production readiness. This report does not state that any of those checks ran.
