## Boundary and caveats

A 5,760-frame base is exactly `.120000 s` at 48 kHz. A 6,719-frame variant is `6719/48000 = 0.139979166666… s`; the LOG's `.139979` reflects ffprobe's rounded display/measurement. Subtracting the exact frame durations gives `959/48000 = 0.019979166666… s` (the LOG also gives measured delta `0.019978999999999997`), which is below `.020 s`. This does not establish exact cutoff equality or behavior at equality. The variant is not a canonical `.120 s` UI fixture: the separate expected duration is only for this scratch probe.
