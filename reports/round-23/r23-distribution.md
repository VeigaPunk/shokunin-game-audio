## Explicitly not measured as an acceptance window

This is a descriptive receipt distribution only. There is **no LUFS acceptance threshold/window** in current source. Null is not a measured loudness value or proof of silence, and no LUFS margin is computable. Spectral bandwidth is recorded but has no acceptance predicate. Centroid is the enforced spectral statistic; no bandwidth threshold should be inferred.

PCM16 is enforced by exact ffprobe `codec_name == pcm_s16le` and `bits_per_sample == 16`; rate/channel require 48000/mono. The clean receipt shows 13 matches, but numerical “distance” to categorical metadata predicates is not defined.
