# Round 16 critic — signed 180 Hz reversal probe

## Claim under review

The declared gap is specific: the existing 180 Hz positive control has first/second section component amplitudes about `.015/.050` FS, while the flat control is about `.036/.036`. A candidate with first/second amplitudes `.050/.015` would exercise the opposite **signed section order** under the existing contrast calculation, unlike an equal-amplitude negative. This is a plausible depth move if the runner actually reports a negative contrast and that is its only failure. It does not establish that prior coverage was absent beyond the inspected round-16 declaration and fixture code.

## Falsification checks / caveats

- `section_contrast` defines `20*log10(second_amplitude / first_amplitude)`, requiring both amplitudes >= `.01` FS and both section RMS values >= `.001` FS. Thus reversal is meaningfully distinct from flatness in the metric: equal sections yield 0 dB, while a true `.050/.015` ordering yields about **−10.46 dB**. The gate rejects either because it tests `delta_db >= 6`, but the signed receipt can distinguish the negative direction. A result showing only “contrast failure” without the signed value, measured amplitude order, `valid=true`, and all other checks passing would not substantiate this move.
- I independently evaluated the declared simple synthesis hypothesis in memory: 4s, 48kHz, mono `round(clip(where(t<2,.050,.015)*sin(2π·180t),-1,1)*32767)` PCM16. Its section FFT-bin amplitudes are approximately `.0499994/.0150004` FS; signed contrast is `−10.45725 dB`; join adjacent delta `.00119022` FS; side-slope mismatch `.00079348` FS/sample; full-wave centroid `277.95 Hz`; peak `.04999`, RMS `.02610`. These calculations suggest the stated contrast should be sole failure under the listed local thresholds, but are **not** runner evidence, a candidate-file check, or independent replay. Quantization and implementation/toolchain details still require the runner receipt.
- The existing component validity floor is `.01` FS, and the proposed amplitudes clear it. Because validity is tied to both fitted 180-Hz amplitude and section RMS, report both section values and `valid`; do not infer validity just from the requested synthesis constants. A phase or boundary artifact could alter join metrics; use decoded PCM, not synthesis intent.
- Sole-failure evidence must check the candidate's format/duration, level, spectral centroid band, outer-loop endpoint and slope, internal-join delta and slope, section validity and signed contrast. Report the generated scratch hash and compare its bytes to the selected candidate. The reversed 180-Hz-only waveform's expected centroid (~278 Hz) is inside the `stem_layer_alt` 100–2000 Hz band, but only actual runner values establish that. Candidate should be distinct from the positive control; no canonical files should be altered.
- Contrast is an FFT-bin component-amplitude ratio for two fixed 2-second halves. It does not measure perceptual loudness, onset masking, phase continuity, spectral balance beyond the selected bin, transitions between sections, or musical intent. A negative ratio is an operational polarity/order test, not evidence of an audible reversal, bad seam, or production defect. The pointwise join proxies likewise do not prove audibility or seamlessness.
- Preserve all canonical hashes and the five existing `_alt` variant records, and independently replay the old modes before attributing an improvement. One synthetic negative alone does not show broad coverage or validate the threshold outside these fixtures. No threshold change is needed or justified here.
