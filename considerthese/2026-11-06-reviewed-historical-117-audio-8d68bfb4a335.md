# Listener profiles and spectral changes

Deutsch’s [A Musical Paradox](https://deutsch.ucsd.edu/pdf/MP-1986_3_275-280.pdf), Spring 1986, reports systematic but individually differing tritone direction judgments in six of eight trained listeners.

Her [spectral variables study](https://deutsch.ucsd.edu/pdf/P%26P-1987_41_563-575.pdf), 1987, finds that envelope height and component amplitudes can modify profiles without abolishing them. Its four preselected listeners do not establish population prevalence, universal direction or behavior with ordinary instruments. This is a refinement, not independent replication; transposition alone does not settle perceived contour.
