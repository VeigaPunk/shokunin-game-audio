# Audio: separate object response from contact

[Van den Doel–Pai](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/labs/lci/papers/docs1998/pai-vanPai96.pdf), manuscript dated July30,1996: impact position changes modal amplitudes within the object's frequency signature. Radiation is approximated, directionality omitted and continuous contact remains future work. This is not the later journal edition.

[FoleyAutomatic](https://www.cs.mcgill.ca/~kry/pubs/foleyautomatic/foleyautomatic.pdf), associated with2001 in the original record: location-dependent resonances combine with separately modeled contact forces sampled at audio rates. Rolling suggests feedback beyond the independent-force approximation. Evaluation is informal and qualitative; convincing isolated impacts do not establish a universal contact model.

The earlier model separates material and position from interaction. The later work extends excitation to sustained contact, while exposing where that separation breaks down. For a prop's sound family, distinguish changes to its response, the implement's contact and the listening scene rather than approving every action from one attractive hit. No listening result.
