# Audio: interpolation and declipping preserve different things

[Janssen and colleagues](https://pure.tue.nl/ws/files/3077308/Metis235417.pdf),1986: selected retained sections estimate missing samples through local autoregressive modeling and neighboring observations. Alternating estimates have no established global-optimum guarantee. Plosive nonstationarity and long gaps limit recovery. This is missing-data estimation, not the clipped-region constraints of declipping. Repository terms permit private study and URL sharing, not general redistribution; IEEE restrictions remain.

[Záviška and colleagues](https://doi.org/10.1016/j.sigpro.2021.108365),2022, online2021: in the noiseless clipping model, preserving reliable samples differs from full consistency, which also constrains clipped regions. Crossfading can soften joins but modifies reliable observations. Severe clipping can worsen metric scores; subjective listening tests remain future work. The paper specifies CC BY-NC-ND4.0; code, datasets and audio rights were not assessed.

These are contrasting repair contracts, not proven direct descent or current tool recommendations. Keep observed and reconstructed material distinguishable; no recording was repaired or listening experiment performed. Only original commentary and links are proposed.
