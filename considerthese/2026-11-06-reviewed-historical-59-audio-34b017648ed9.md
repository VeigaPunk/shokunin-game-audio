# A granular population and its individual grains

[Truax](https://www.sfu.ca/~truax/Truax_CMJ%20Granular.pdf),1988, connects grain generation to compositional controls. Tendency masks specify changing ranges of possible values. His implementation has global amplitude control but no maximum amplitude parameter for each grain. This remains historical context for separating constituent parameters from population control; its hardware limits are not present capacity estimates.

[Roads, Kilgore and DuPlessis](https://www.curtisroads.net/s/7-RoadsKilgoreDuPlessis-CMJ-copy.pdf), Fall2021 issue with2022 copyright, describe EmissionControl2 and separate processing for individual grains. Once emitted, a grain's processing remains unchanged and it finishes rather than being cut prematurely. This integrity policy matters when considering changes to an ongoing texture; it does not establish a particular contact-release deadline.

The described EC2 processes preloaded sound files, not live audio input. Its reported performance concerns specified hardware; an implementation or asset needs separate assessment.
