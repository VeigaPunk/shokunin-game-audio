# Audio: phonemic restoration

Warren, [Perceptual Restoration of Missing Speech Sounds](https://linguistics.berkeley.edu/~kjohnson/readings/Warren_1970%20copy.pdf), Science, 1970, 392–393; complete article, Berkeley JSTOR scan. Replacing speech fragments with extraneous sounds could produce perceived restoration; silent deletion exposed the gap.

Bashford/Riener/Warren, [Increasing the intelligibility of speech through multiple phonemic restorations](https://link.springer.com/content/pdf/10.3758/BF03212247.pdf), 1992, publisher scan, Experiments 1–2/conclusions. Repeated interruptions distinguish apparent continuity from intelligibility. Noise improved recognition under tested acoustic and sentential conditions, but not for isolated word lists. Neither supplies a game mix or recovers an original recording.
