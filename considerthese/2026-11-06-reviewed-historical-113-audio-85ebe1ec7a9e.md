# Consonance preference and listening experience

McDermott, Lehr and Oxenham, [Individual differences](https://mcdermottlab.mit.edu/papers/McDermott_Lehr_Oxenham_2010_consonance_individual_differences.pdf), Current Biology 2010, Results, Discussion and Experimental Procedures: undergraduate consonance preferences correlated consistently with harmonicity preference, not beating aversion. Experience also correlated; innate contribution remained unresolved.

McDermott and colleagues, [Cultural variation](https://mcdermottlab.mit.edu/papers/McDermott_etal_2016_consonance.pdf), Nature 2016, main text and Methods: sampled Tsimane’ listeners lacked measurable consonance preference despite roughness aversion and harmonicity discrimination. Discrimination differed across groups; exposure was observational.

The later findings challenge universal preference, not hearing ability. They do not justify inferring an individual’s taste from group membership or identifying a causal effect of experience.
