# Material judgments and size

Klatzky/Pai/Krotkov, [Perception of Material from Contact Sounds](https://www.cs.ubc.ca/labs/lci/papers/docs2000/pai-KlaPai00.pdf), 2000 UBC prepublication manuscript: synthesized clamped bars produced stronger decay than frequency effects. Unequal manipulated ranges and nonequivalent logarithmic changes limit generalization.

Giordano/McAdams, [Material identification of real impact sounds](https://www.mcgill.ca/mpcl/files/mpcl/blg_smc_2006_jasa.pdf), JASA 119, February 2006: real plates yielded broad-category recognition alongside size-related confusions within categories. Subsequent modeling uses the largest listener cluster. Multiple acoustic descriptors explain broad distinctions; no unique perceptual cause follows, nor guaranteed recognition of bells.
