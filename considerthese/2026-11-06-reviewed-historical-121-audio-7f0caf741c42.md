# Clapping: perception and airflow

Repp, [Clapping](https://files.eric.ed.gov/fulltext/ED278066.pdf), Haskins SR86/87, 1986, General Discussion and notes: hand configuration relates to spectra, but cross-clapper identification remains inconclusive. Casual observation, confounded individual differences, filtered frequencies and rate/loudness variation limit inference. Suboptimal recording did not show serious artifacts. ERIC report scan, not the 1987 journal edition.

Fletcher, [Hand clap model](https://www.acoustics.asn.au/journal/2013/2013_41_2_Fletcher_paper.pdf), Acoustics Australia 41(2), August 2013, 165–168, complete article: simplified airflow distinguishes flat and cupped impacts. Shock generation depends on closure geometry. This semi-quantitative account does not replicate listener identification.
