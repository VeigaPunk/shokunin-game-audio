# Repetition and song perception

Deutsch, Henthorn and Lapidis, [Speech to song](https://deutsch.ucsd.edu/pdf/JASA-2011_129_2245-2252.pdf), JASA 2011, §II: exact repetitions became songlike; tested fractional transpositions did not. Scope: one phrase and musically trained listeners. UCSD author complimentary copy; ASA copyright.

Loutrari, [A phrase in a loop](https://journals.sagepub.com/doi/pdf/10.1177/10298649241262672?download=true), Musicae Scientiae 2025, copyright 2024, Task I/Discussion: tested upward major seconds received slightly higher songlikeness ratings than exact repetition. Different stimuli, listeners and rating schedules prevent a simple replication claim; mechanism remains unresolved.
