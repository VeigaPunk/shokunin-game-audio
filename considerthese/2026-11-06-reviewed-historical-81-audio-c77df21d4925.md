# Audio: exposure and timing judgments

[Fujisaki et al.](https://www.pallier.org/lectures/tpexp3/projects/Fujisaki.recalibration.audiovisual.nn2004.pdf), 2004 advance-online layout: repeated fixed audiovisual lag shifted subjective simultaneity. Selected results and methods show small samples including authors; this is not a game timing tolerance.

[Van der Burg, Alais and Cass](https://research.vu.nl/ws/portalfiles/portal/416318388/Rapid_recalibration_to_audiovisual_asynchrony_follows_the_physical_not_the_perceived_temporal_order.pdf), 2018 version of record: rapid recalibration followed preceding physical order rather than reported order. Interleaved judgment tasks may activate different decision criteria; prolonged and rapid effects need not share mechanisms. The later distinction sharpens interpretation, not a universal replacement for prolonged adaptation research.

For cue review, distinguish artistic placement, delivered timing and exposure history; these readings do not calibrate a device. Selected passages only, no experiment.
