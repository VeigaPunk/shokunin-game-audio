## Loudness: deliberately no acceptance window

FFmpeg `ebur128` integrated LUFS is parsed only as a **reported** value; nonfinite or `<=−70 LUFS` readings are recorded `null`. Six short SFX (`ui`, `ui_alt`, `impact`, `impact_alt`, `movement`, `movement_alt`) are null in the clean receipt; four longer fixtures are ambience −30.7, ambience_alt −24.7, stem −15.8, stem_sections −24.7 LUFS. Null is a gated-floor/uninterpretable result in this implementation, **not** a measured −70 LUFS level, silence, or a pass/fail target. There is **no declared or enforced loudness acceptance window** to justify. The finite four-value range is descriptive only; RMS dBFS and the linear RMS floor answer a separate question. [FFmpeg ebur128](https://ffmpeg.org/ffmpeg-filters.html#ebur128); [EBU R128](https://tech.ebu.ch/publications/r128).

## Decision boundary and untested cases

Normal ten-file replay tests positive cases. The flat30 control isolates a below-6-dB contrast; slope control isolates a large slope mismatch despite matching endpoints; deliberately damaged ambience fails centroid **and** endpoint together, not either in isolation. Wrong sample rate/channel, bit depth, duration edge, peak/RMS boundary, centroid edges, numeric variant OR threshold, section count/floors and LUFS reporting behavior lack dedicated negative controls in this round. In particular, `section_contrast()` splits samples and indexes FFT bin 60 **before** checking the 192000-sample predicate; odd or very short malformed inputs may raise rather than produce a JSON failure receipt. Exact count is a condition for a passing result, not proven graceful rejection of every wrong count. The controls far beyond threshold do not establish sensitivity near the edge. The table improves disclosure and auditability of *existing* fixture rules; it does not tune or validate their numeric values, infer a population distribution, or support perceptual/mastering/rights claims.
